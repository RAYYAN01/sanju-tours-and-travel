Frequently asked questions

How do I book a vehicle with Sanju Tours & Travels?

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 73381 48518, or submit the booking form on this website. We confirm vehicle availability the same day and send an itemised quotation with the per-km rate, driver bata and daily kilometre minimum before you pay anything.

How is outstation pricing calculated? Are there hidden charges?

Outstation trips are billed on a fixed per-kilometre rate — from ₹11/km for a Swift Dzire sedan up to ₹45/km for a 33-seater tourist coach — with a 300 km/day minimum plus a fixed driver bata per day. Tolls, permits and parking are paid at actuals. There are no other charges.

Do you provide airport transfers to Hubballi Airport (HBX)?

Yes. We run 24/7 airport taxi pickups and drops for Hubballi Airport (HBX) on Gokul Road, tracking your flight so the driver is ready on arrival, covering Hubli, Dharwad, Navanagar, Vidyanagar and nearby towns.

Can I book a Tempo Traveller or coach for weddings and group events?

Yes. We run a 12-seater Force Tempo Traveller and a 33-seater tourist coach, and coordinate timing with railway arrivals at Hubballi Junction, airport drops and hotel-to-venue convoys.
